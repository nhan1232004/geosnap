# 📥 **Download google-services.json - Step by Step**

## **Cách làm (2 phút):**

1. **Mở Firebase Console:**
   ```
   https://console.firebase.google.com/project/geosnap-4dd7a/settings/general
   ```

2. **Scroll xuống → Tìm "Android apps"**

3. **Nếu chưa có Android app:**
   - Click "Add app"
   - Chọn "Android"
   - Package name: `com.geosnap.app`
   - SHA-1 (optional, skip)
   - Click "Register app"

4. **Click vào Android app → "google-services.json"**

5. **Download file**

6. **Copy nội dung file đó → paste vào chat/save somewhere**
